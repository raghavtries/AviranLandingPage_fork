Sheet 03Five stages

The same loop, stage by stage.

Understand, onboard, and maintain, broken into the five stages you see in the product. Each stage leaves an artifact you can read, and nothing ships without your approval.

Learn the product once.

Aviran inspects your documentation, schemas, APIs, repositories, and sample deployments. The result is a confirmed product contract: what’s configurable, what’s supported, and what needs engineering.

  • Identifies configurable prompts, policies, tools, and workflows
  • Maps supported integrations, field mappings, and permissions
  • Skippable when you already hand us a complete contract

Extract requirements, not assumptions.

Aviran reads client documents, call transcripts, screen recordings, policies, Slack threads, and sample data. It extracts structured, cited requirements and routes open questions to the right person.

  • Every requirement is traceable to its source
  • Missing or conflicting information is flagged and routed automatically
  • Blocker owners and status are tracked until resolved

Generate the customer-specific build.

Aviran combines your product contract with the client’s confirmed requirements. The result includes configuration, field mappings, workflows, and acceptance criteria for testing. When the client uses a platform you haven’t integrated before, Aviran helps wire that integration too.

  • Config, mappings, and workflows generated from the product contract
  • Acceptance tests and an implementation plan produced alongside the build
  • Every artifact traces back to a source requirement

Prove it works before it ships.

Aviran checks the proposed configuration against your product contract for schema compatibility, permission correctness, expected behavior, and regressions. Each check produces evidence beyond a simple pass or fail.

  • Contract compliance and schema checks
  • Behavioral validation against acceptance criteria
  • Failed checks include proposed remediation

Approve once. Maintain forever.

First, Aviran presents the plan, risks, and rollback path for operator approval. Then Aviran launches the customer. After launch, Aviran suggests supported changes for approval before they ship. Examples include policy updates, field mappings, and routing rules.

  • Full plan, risk, and rollback surfaced before launch
  • Post-launch changes are suggested, then approved, never silent
  • Unsupported work escalates to engineering with full context
Sheet 04Diligence

Questions.

Q1Why wouldn’t we build this internally?

Your product quality is important, and any time your engineers can spend on the core product is a win.

But we know that deployment is often a lengthy process, involving work that’s quite different from building your core product.

For each client, figuring out their business logic can involve extensive requirements intake, configuration generation, validation, approvals, rollout, rollback, and auditability. And as their needs change, much of this work continues well beyond the initial deployment.

Aviran helps automate this entire onboarding and maintenance process, giving your engineering team more time to focus on improving and growing your product.

Q2What can Aviran actually change without approval?

Everything can be set to on-approval only, and independently configured for different scenarios.

Q3What happens when the customer is the blocker?

When this happens Aviran first identifies who owns each blocker. Then Aviran tries asking the owner directly. If there’s no result, Aviran turns the answer into an implementation record and gives suggestions on what next steps to take from here.

Q4What if a client runs on a system with no API?

We leverage tools to create endpoints for you, so your agent can still call into that system. Aviran also maps how the client works there, so your FDEs know what the integration needs.
